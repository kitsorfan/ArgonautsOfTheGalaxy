# Voice credits

The game's voices are synthetic speech, made with `npm run voice` (see the README's **Voices**): the
trailer's narration (`trailer/`) and the story's voice-over (`game/`). The trailer's end card credits
the engine too.

| What | By | License | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Speech model: Pocket TTS | Kyutai | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | https://huggingface.co/kyutai/pocket-tts |
| Speech model: Chatterbox | Resemble AI | [MIT](https://opensource.org/license/mit) | https://huggingface.co/ResembleAI/chatterbox |
